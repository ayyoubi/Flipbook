Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

1. Data Collection

We don't collect, track, or care about your personal data. This app is just for laughs, jokes, and hanging out with friends in voice channels.

2. Cookies & Tracking

There are no tracking cookies, analytics, or shady background trackers. What happens in the voice call stays in the voice call.

3. Third-Party Hosting

The app is hosted on GitHub Pages and runs inside Discord's secure environment. Whatever Discord handles for your session is up to them, but we aren't saving any of your info.

4. Contact

If you have any questions or just want to complain about the jokes, reach out via Discord.
